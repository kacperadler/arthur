# arthur silent

Usage: `arthur silent`; `arthur silent on ['<the work; its stop condition>'] [--until <HH:MM>]`; `arthur silent off`; `arthur silent ask`. On a channel: `/silent`, and `/silent on <the work> [--until <HH:MM>]`, `/silent on <the work> [--until <HH:MM>] [--usage <percent>] [--tickets <id>,...] --recommend` or `/silent off`. Comes with the `orchestration` plugin.

The silent mode (roadmap entry 47): for work the owner delegates and wants to hear of only at its end. While it is on, the owner gets the reply to his own message, the end of the work, its stop condition met, and a decision only he can make; the rest waits, and is summed up when it goes off. It always has an end, at which it goes off on its own and the owner is told ("Its end", below).

- `arthur silent` (and `/silent`) toggles it, answering the new state.
- `on` turns it on, `silent: on until <HH:MM> on <day> <date>`, its end, with the work and its stop condition when given (a limit of time, money or tickets). `--until <HH:MM>` sets the end, a time of day in `core.timeZone`, at its next occurrence; without it the end is 24 hours after this `on`. While it is on already, it keeps since when and replaces the work and the end, and a grant (below) given before goes unless this `on` gives one.
- `off` turns it off: `silent: off, meanwhile: <what happened>`, built without the model from the events since it went on: the jobs that ended, by state (the first word of the reason: `finished`, `failed`, `stopped`, `limit`, or `blocked`), with their ids; the tickets merged; each notice held, one line each. `silent: off, nothing happened meanwhile` when there was none. While it is off already, it answers `silent: off` and changes nothing.
- `ask` lets the reply of the turn that runs it reach the owner (a decision), and silent stays on: `silent: on; the reply of this turn goes to the owner`. It counts for the session that runs it alone, the coordinator's or one project agent's: a reply of another session stays held and does not use it up (AR0713). Run outside every session (a terminal), it counts for the coordinator's. While it is off: `silent: off`.
- An `--until` that is no time of day, `HH:MM` from `00:00` to `23:59`, is refused and nothing is written: `arthur silent: refused: --until <value>: a time of day, HH:MM`, exit 1; on a channel the reply is `silent: refused: --until <value>: a time of day, HH:MM`.
- Anything else, `--until` without its value or given twice included, prints the usage line and exits 2; on a channel the reply is that line.

The coordinator runs `arthur silent on '<the work; its stop condition>' --until <HH:MM>`, the time its limit of time ends, by itself when the owner delegates work with a stop condition, `arthur silent off` when that work is done or its stop condition met, and `arthur silent ask` for a decision; its role says what counts as each. A project agent runs `arthur silent ask` in a turn that asks the owner for a decision, as its role says.

## Its end

The mode never stays on past its end (AR0739: turned on for a night's work, it stayed on for six days, and every reply to a job's end, a review, a CI run or a merge was held while the owner waited for them).

- The end is the `until` of the state: the `--until` of the last `on`, else 24 hours after it; a grant's `--until` is that same end. A state an Arthur before AR0739 wrote, without `until`, ends 24 hours after its `since`.
- `arthur run` checks it in each sweep of the outbox (every 500 ms, `arthur coordinator --help`, "The coordinator's inbox"), before that sweep's replies. At the end or past it, the mode goes off as by `arthur silent off`, the state file and each session's `silent-ask` removed, and one `notice` goes to the owner, built without the model: `silent: off on its own, its end <HH:MM> on <day> <date> came (on since <HH:MM> on <day> <date>, for: <the work>); meanwhile: <what happened>`, the summary `off` answers (`nothing happened meanwhile` when there was none; `for: nothing named` when no work was given). The replies of that sweep and after are sent; what was held stays in `outbox/held/`.
- The state file is taken by a rename before anyone tells: of the end and an `arthur silent off` at the same instant, one gets the state and tells what happened, and the other finds the mode off (`silent: off`, or no notice). A second sweep tells nothing more.
- A grant ends with the mode: an ask is answered by recommendation only before the mode's end, also while `arthur run` is down and has not turned it off yet. A grant bounded only by `--usage` or `--tickets` so ends 24 hours after its `on`, unless that `on` gave an `--until`.
- A state file that does not parse has no end to read and stays on until `arthur silent off` removes it; the context names it on every turn ("The state", below).

## Go with my recommendation

`/silent on ... --recommend` records the owner's grant (AR0583), for when he delegates work saying "go with your recommendation": while it holds, an `arthur decision ask` with `--recommend` is answered in the same write by that recommendation, the option it numbers or its words, instead of waiting for him. The answer's words are `by recommendation: <grant id>`, it carries no `--picked`, and the ask prints a second line, `<id> answered by recommendation (<grant id>): <decided>; act on it as on the owner's answer, and say so in your reply` (`arthur decision --help`).

Only the owner gives it, typing it on his phone (AR0664): `arthur silent on ... --recommend` in a shell, the coordinator's included, is refused (`arthur silent: refused: --recommend is the owner's grant: he gives it on his phone, /silent on <the work> --until <HH:MM> --recommend`, exit 1, nothing written), and so is saying it in chat: the coordinator asks him to type it.

- The grant carries at least one bound, and holds while every bound given holds and the mode is on: `--until <HH:MM>`, a time of day in `core.timeZone`, until its next occurrence (exclusive), which is the mode's own end too ("Its end", above); `--usage <percent>`, a whole percent from 1 to 100, while the highest used window of the agent's usage report (`models.usage`, the models plugin, `arthur usage --help`) is under it, never with no report or when its last fetch failed; `--tickets <id>,...`, only an ask whose `--ticket` is one of them.
- It never answers a plan question (kind `plan`: plan questions always go to the owner, AR-D603), a merge question (kind `merge`: a merge, `--what --unreviewed` or `--no-ci`, or `arthur take-main`, each the owner's own decision), a budget question (kind `budget`, which `--budget 0` takes), a class's proposal, or an ask without `--recommend`. A question an approved class answers by policy, or that two classes match, is not answered by it.
- `/silent on` answers `silent: on until <HH:MM> on <day> <date>; the owner's grant G<n>: ...`, the grant in words; the context names it as long as it holds. Its id, `G<n>`, is the number of the event `silent-grant` `{ work, until?, usage?, tickets? }` that records it in the event log. `off`, the mode's own end, and an `on` without `--recommend` from the shell or the phone, end it.
- Refused (`silent: refused: <why>`, nothing written): no bound; an `--until` other than `HH:MM`; a `--usage` other than a whole percent from 1 to 100, or one with no plugin providing `models.usage`; a `--tickets` that is not ticket ids, comma-separated without spaces. `--usage` or `--tickets` without `--recommend`, or a flag without its value or given twice, is answered with the usage.

## The state

`<home>/orchestration/silent.json`: `{ since, seq, work, until, grant? }`, since when (an ISO time), the number of the last event when it went on, the work, when it ends on its own (an ISO time; a state written before AR0739 has none), and the grant, `{ id, until?, usage?, tickets? }` (`until` an ISO time). It is written to a temporary name and renamed, so a reader never sees half of it, and it survives a restart of `arthur run` and of the coordinator. No file means off. A file that does not parse, or holds anything else (a `since` or `until` that is no time, a grant without its id, or a bound of another type, included), counts as on with no grant and no end: the context says so, naming the file, and `arthur silent off` removes it, answering `silent: off, <file> did not parse, so what happened meanwhile is unknown`. Going off, the file is renamed to `silent.json.<pid>` and removed.

## What it holds

- A reply of a turn holding no owner message (a job's end, a review, a schedule entry, only messages from Arthur, `[Arthur]` or `[Arthur: <plugin>]`) is not emitted: `arthur run` moves it to the `outbox/held/` of the session that wrote it, `<home>/orchestration/outbox/held/` for the coordinator and `<home>/orchestration/agents/<project>/outbox/held/` for a project agent, instead of sending it, where it stays, like `outbox/sent/`, for 7 days. It is sent when that turn ran `arthur silent off` (the mode is off by the time the reply is written) or `arthur silent ask` (which writes the time to `silent-ask` in the folder of the session that ran it, `<home>/orchestration/` for the coordinator and `<home>/orchestration/agents/<project>/` for a project agent; the first reply of that session written after it is sent, and the mark goes). `on` from off, `off` and the mode's own end remove the mark each session left behind; a removed agent's goes to the coordinator at `arthur run`'s start (`arthur coordinator --help`, "Project agents").
- The reply of a turn holding an owner message is sent as always; the coordinator's role has it answer that message only, not the wakes beside it.
- A notice: each channel asks `call("coordinator.silent", notice)` before it sends one, and sends it only when the answer is not `true`. While silent is on, every notice is held but the one of a job blocked on the owner (`blocked: "<job id>"` in it), which is a decision, and the one of a high-impact answer recorded (`decision: "<id>"` in it, `arthur decision --help`, "Answering"), which he must see. Held notices are not kept apart: `off`, and the notice of the mode's own end, read them from the event log.
- What it lets through goes as every send: in quiet hours without a notification.

## The context

While it is on, the coordinator's context (`arthur coordinator context`) and each project agent's (`arthur coordinator context <project>`) have a `## Silent` section after `## Time`: `On since <HH:MM> on <day> <date>, for: <the work; its stop condition>. It ends on its own at <HH:MM> on <day> <date>, and Arthur tells the owner.`, or `for: nothing named; the owner turned it on.` when no work was given, and with a grant a second line, `While it holds, the owner's grant G<n>: ...`; with a broken state file, `On: <file> does not parse, so it counts as on; arthur silent off removes it.`
