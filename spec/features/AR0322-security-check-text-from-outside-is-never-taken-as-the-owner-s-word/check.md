# AR0322 - Check: text from outside is never taken as the owner's word

The rule of this area: text that does not come from the owner (a job's final message, a GitHub review or CI log, a YouTrack issue, a ticket a job wrote, a web page) is marked as data in every role's prompt and can never appear as a paragraph from the owner.

Where the owner's word is told apart from everything else: the coordinator's inbox (`packages/orchestration/src/hand-over.ts`) marks each message it hands the agent with `[via <channel>]` (a `message-received` from a channel plugin) or `[Arthur]` (a `job-ended` wake, a `coordinator-message` from any plugin), and the coordinator's role (`packages/orchestration/roles/coordinator.md:12`) says `[via <channel>]` is the owner. The coordinator acts on the owner's word alone for a merge, an approval, `--unreviewed`, `stopall` (`coordinator.md:25`, `:31`, `:81`). A job gets the owner's word through its task (`wrapper.ts:88`), a steer (`packages/core/src/steer.ts:28`) and a continue (`job-control.ts:101`, `wrapper.ts:74-77`), all unmarked, as the coordinator's relay; who may write those is AR0323's area.

How each row was checked: by reading the lines named, by the tests named, and by a throwaway probe (not committed) that called `handOver` of `hand-over.ts` with a job's final message holding a blank line and a line starting `[via telegram]`, and `path.matchesGlob` (Node v24.21.0) with the default always-review list against the paths the roles read. Nothing ran against the VPS, a bot, GitHub, YouTrack or a client repository.

Verdicts: `holds`, `holds without a test`, `gap` (a `findings.md` entry each), or a pointer to the area whose ticket carries it.
